About Us

We are BSIT - 1D (opaw club), a group of friends bound by laughter, memories, and a shared love for adventure. Our journey started 1 year ago, and we’ve been through thick and thin together.

Our bond is built on support, respect, and trust. From celebrating milestones to lazy Sundays, every moment strengthens our friendship.

Through laughter, tears, inside jokes, and shared experiences, we’ve created a family that goes beyond friendship.

We’re more than just a group of friends — we’re a barkada, here for the long haul. Here’s to many more years of memories and adventures!
